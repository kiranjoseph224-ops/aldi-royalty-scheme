# Aldi Royalty Scheme — Business Case Proposal

## Overview
Developed a business case for an Aldi rewards app to compete with Lidl, Dunnes and Tesco, as part of a team project. The proposal covered infrastructure analysis, Business Model Canvas, Ethics Canvas, GDPR compliance and proposed customer-level KPIs.

## My Role
**Team Member (Planet Profit)**

- Created the Ethics Canvas, identifying ethical risks and proposing mitigations
- Conducted GDPR compliance analysis covering consent, data subject rights, retention and cross-border transfers
- Contributed to the Business Model Canvas
- Proposed 4 customer-level KPIs to measure loyalty programme performance
- Contributed to the infrastructure analysis of Aldi's legacy-to-cloud integration gap

## Technologies & Frameworks Used
- Business Model Canvas
- Ethics Canvas
- GDPR Framework
- Infrastructure Analysis
- KPI Development

## Key Contributions
- Identified ethical risks including algorithmic bias in personalised promotions, the digital divide affecting non-smartphone users, and third-party API failure
- Proposed mitigations including opt-in consent, a physical alternative for non-digital customers, transparent pricing, and clear data usage reporting
- Analysed stakeholder impact across 5 groups: rewards app users, non-app customers, Aldi employees, management and store managers
- Identified Aldi's main infrastructure gap: fragile integration between legacy on-premise systems and modern cloud services

## Proposed KPIs
- Customer-level metrics
- Promotion tracking
- Segment-based forecasting
